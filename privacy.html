Bucklog privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

What data the app accesses

Bucklog requests the Google drive.file scope. It can only access Google Sheets you created with Bucklog or explicitly chose in Google Picker. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.

In those sheets, the app reads and writes expense entries: date, the name you entered in the app, description, category, amount and currency. It also reads your Google account email to show which account is connected.

Where data is stored

Entries are stored in your Google Sheet and in a local database on your device. Bucklog has no servers. Your data isn't sent to the developer or to any third party. The app contacts only Google APIs, and public exchange-rate services (National Bank of Poland, ECB) for currency rates, which receive no personal data.

Sharing

Who can see your entries is decided entirely by the sharing settings of your Google Sheet.

Removing access and data

Uninstall the app to delete the local data. Revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. The sheet remains yours, and you can delete it in Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Bucklog's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
